Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and login credentials, along with the expense records you enter — amount, category, date, and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate Spendly: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating the spending summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and are only accessible through your authenticated session. We take reasonable measures to protect this data from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) solely to operate the application, and they process data only on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account settings page.
